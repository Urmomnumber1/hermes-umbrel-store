Instructions

Download Sour Player

Sour Player is the music player for Hermes Music: music videos, Group Play, Sour Radio, profiles and requests. It plays the music from the shared library.

WindowsSour-Player-win-x64.exe Linux (any)AppImage Linux (Ubuntu / Mint / Debian).deb Mac (Apple chip, 2020+).dmg - test Mac (Intel).dmg - test
  1. Not sure which you have? If your computer has a Start button with the Windows logo, pick Windows. Almost every PC and laptop from a shop runs Windows. Linux is something you would have installed on purpose.
  2. Windows: open the downloaded file. If Windows says the app is unrecognised, click More info > Run anyway.
  3. Linux AppImage: right-click it > Properties > allow running as a program (or chmod +x), then open it. .deb: double-click it, or sudo apt install ./Sour-Player-linux-amd64.deb.
  4. In Sour Player: add your music server (Navidrome), then Settings > General > Music videos > Hermes Music address: this page's address.
  5. It updates itself: when a new version is ready, an Update button shows up in the player bar.
Request a song
  1. Type the song name and the artist.
  2. Press Enter. It shows queued, then downloading, then added.
  3. It lands in your music folder with its album, year and cover. Navidrome picks it up on its next scan.
mr brightside the killers
queued - it will show up in the library soon
[added]The Killers - Mr. Brightside (Hot Fuss (2004))

/album
  1. Type /album, then the artist and the album name.
  2. The tracklist is looked up (iTunes, then Deezer) and every track downloads one by one.
  3. The line shows progress like downloading 4/12. Songs you already have are skipped.
/album the killers hot fuss
[downloading 4/12]album: The Killers - Hot Fuss

example

/artist
  1. Type /artist and the artist's name.
  2. Their newest albums download (how many, and whether singles and EPs count, is set in Config).
  3. Under the line you can see how many albums, EPs and singles are finished.
/artist daft punk
[downloading 31/58]artist: Daft Punk - 6 releasesalbums 3/5 · EPs 0/1

Spotify links
  1. In Spotify, tap Share → Copy link on a song, album, playlist or artist.
  2. Paste the link into the box and press Enter. No Spotify account needed.
https://open.spotify.com/playlist/37i9dQZF1DX...
[downloading 7/50]playlist: Spotify - Today's Top Hits

example

/duplicate
  1. Type /duplicate. It checks the whole library and lists copies of the same song (same artist, title and length). Nothing is changed yet.
  2. Check the list. The keep file is the best-quality copy.
  3. Type /duplicate confirm (add your PIN if you set one: /duplicate confirm 1234).
  4. The extra copies move to the hidden .duplicates folder in your music folder. Delete that folder in the Files app when you're sure.
/duplicate
keep The Killers/Hot Fuss/02 Mr. Brightside.mp3 (320 kbps)
remove The Killers - Mr. Brightside.mp3 (160 kbps)
1 song has copies, 1 file would be removed

example

Skip the queue: /now, /bump
  1. Everything waiting has a queue number, like [queued #3].
  2. /now and that number moves it to the front, e.g. /now 3. For an album, artist or playlist, all its remaining tracks go first.
  3. Or use /bump and part of the name, e.g. /bump hot fuss.
  4. Pushed requests show a ⚡. The next worker that finishes its current song takes it, usually within a minute.
[queued #3]blinding lights
/now 3
⚡ #3 blinding lights is next - the next free worker takes it
[downloading #1 ⚡]The Weeknd - Blinding Lights

example

Music videos: /video
  1. Type /video and a song that's already in your library, e.g. /video mr brightside the killers.
  2. Hermes Music finds its official music video on YouTube (not lyric videos, audio uploads or live versions). Nothing is downloaded.
  3. In the custom Feishin a video button appears in the player bar while that song plays. It opens the video next to the song.
  4. Before a video is used, Hermes Music checks by sound that it's the same recording as your song, so fan edits and other songs are skipped.
  5. Still the wrong video? Press Wrong video under it in Feishin (or type /video wrong mr brightside) and the next best one is tried. Or pick it yourself: /video mr brightside | https://youtu.be/...
  6. The video plays muted, in step with the song (like YouTube Music). Hermes Music lines it up by itself in the background by matching the sound (the video's audio is only fetched for that and deleted right after). If you open the video before that's done, it snaps into sync a moment later.
  7. Rarely a little off? Nudge it with the -0.5s / +0.5s buttons under the video and press Save.
  8. /video list shows all of them, /video remove SONG deletes one.
/video mr brightside the killers
🎬 The Killers - Mr. Brightside now has a music video: The Killers - Mr. Brightside (Official Music Video)

example

Group Play (in Feishin)
  1. Group Play works like a Spotify Jam and lives in the custom Feishin: the people icon in the player bar. Everyone hears the same song at the same moment.
  2. Host: type your name, press Start and share the 5-letter code (Copy invite copies a message to send). Your Feishin plays the music. The group starts with an empty queue; the first song anyone adds starts playing.
  3. Guests: type your name and the code, press Join. Add songs with right-click > Add to group queue. Everyone sees the shared queue with album covers and the name of whoever added each song.
  4. Guests can always remove the songs they added. When the host turns on Let guests control playback, guests can also play/pause, skip, play a song now, move one up next or remove any song, right from the Group Play window.
  5. The host can remove people from the group.
  6. Groups playing now: open groups are listed on the Group Play screen, so friends can join with one click. The host can turn off Show in the group list to need the code instead.
  7. Add a profile picture next to your name in Group Play; it shows in the group instead of your first letter.
  8. Everyone needs the Hermes Music address in Feishin (Settings > General > Music videos) and access to the same Navidrome server.
  9. The group ends when the host presses End group.
Request from Feishin
  1. In the custom Feishin, the + button in the player bar opens Request music: pick Song, Album or Artist, type it (or paste a Spotify link) and press Request.
  2. It goes into the same queue as this page, shows who asked, and the window lists recent requests with their status.
  3. Feishin's Home page shows Your most played: the songs you play most on that computer, even if friends share the same music server login.
Status words
  1. queued waiting for its turn
  2. downloading being found, downloaded and tagged
  3. added saved to the library (or already in the library, so skipped)
  4. failed the reason is shown next to it. With the Downtify backup on, it was tried there too.
Other commands
  1. /help list the commands in the terminal
  2. /queue show queue counts
  3. /config open the settings
  4. /clear clear finished requests from the screen (on this device)
  5. /rescan re-read the music folder now (it also does this by itself every 30 minutes)
  6. /issues check the library for songs with missing tags, no cover or low quality; /fix 3 re-tags number 3 and adds its cover, /upgrade 3 downloads a better copy
  7. /follow the killers new releases from that artist download by themselves (/following lists them, /unfollow NAME stops)
  8. New songs come with synced lyrics when LRCLIB has them (saved next to the song for Navidrome and Sour Player).
